John Personal Assistant

Home Privacy Policy

John Personal Assistant is a private, personal AI assistant built and used by a single individual, its developer and owner. It is not a public product, it has no public sign-up, and it is not offered to other users.

What it does

The assistant helps its owner stay organised by answering questions and preparing short daily briefings about the owner's own information. To do this, it connects to the owner's own Google accounts with read-only access:

  • Personal Google account: read-only access to Gmail, Google Drive and Google Docs.
  • Work Google account: read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar.

For example, the owner can ask "what is my next meeting?" or "did anything important arrive in the last day?", and the assistant looks up the information and replies privately to the owner.

What it does not do

  • It cannot send, delete, or modify email, files, documents or calendar events through these Google connections.
  • It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • It is not available to anyone other than its owner.

Control and privacy

The owner keeps full control of the connected Google accounts and can revoke the assistant's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Full details of how Google data is accessed, used, stored and shared are in the Privacy Policy.
